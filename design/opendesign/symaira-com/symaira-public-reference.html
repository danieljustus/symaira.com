Architecture / trust

No black box where a boundary will do.

Symaira is designed to make the important questions easy to ask: where does data live, which component acts, and what can you inspect?

DataLocal-first storage paths are explicit. Product behavior depends on your deployment, not a hidden hosted layer.
SourceRepositories, release notes, and install instructions are the starting point for verification.
StatusRelease and availability labels stay deliberately factual. Unavailable does not get dressed up as “coming soon”.
ControlHuman decisions remain visible in the workflow—especially when automation reaches beyond a local boundary.
