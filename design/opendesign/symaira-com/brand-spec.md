## Observed rules
1. Warm-black technical canvas with champagne/sand as a sparse signal color.
2. Blueprint grid and atmospheric glows stay behind content and never carry meaning.
3. Generous readable sections, compact deliberate headings, and 4/8/12/16/24/32/48 rhythm.
4. Glass-like panels are reserved for navigation and elevated product surfaces.
5. Product claims stay literal: open-source/self-hosted positioning is visible; hosted Pro is not promised.

**Summary:** Symaira presents open, local-first tools as a calm technical system: warm-black clarity, champagne signal, and human-readable boundaries.
